Our work

Low-field brain MRI scanners are far cheaper than conventional systems and can be moved to the patient, but they yield much lower signal and resolution. Much of our work is about closing that gap with neural networks, and validating to what extent measures — from regional volumes to brain networks — agree across field strengths.
